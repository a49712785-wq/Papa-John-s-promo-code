Papa John's Pizza Promo Code: Carryout vs. Delivery

Both order types qualify for the same percentage-off codes, like SM25 and TAKE25DEAL. The real difference is the delivery fee. It's charged separately and typically isn't reduced by a code, so carryout usually nets slightly more savings on the same order total.

Delivery orders can also carry a small order fee below a certain cart minimum, plus an optional driver tip, neither of which a promo code touches. Ordering through papajohns.com or the app directly avoids the extra service fees that third-party apps like DoorDash or Uber Eats typically add on top — those platforms also don't accept Papa John's own promo codes, since the order isn't placed on Papa John's system.

New Customers vs. Papa Rewards Members

New customers usually come out ahead with signup discounts, up to 30% off. Existing Rewards members usually beat public codes with in-account offers instead. Either way, run the numbers on your actual cart. A 25% public code can occasionally out-save a smaller rewards discount on a large order.

On a $30 order, for example, a 30% new-customer code saves $9, while a Papa Rewards account offer like a $9.99 large pizza could save more or less depending on the menu price it's replacing. The signup discount is a one-time perk tied to your first account order, while Papa Rewards keeps paying out on every order afterward through points, Papa Dough, and a birthday reward — so it's worth joining even right after using the new-customer code.

Teacher, Military & Student Discounts at Papa John's

Teachers, military members, and students can access a separate tier, often around 25% off, by verifying eligibility with a valid ID or an ITIC card at checkout. This discount is restricted to participating locations, and exclusions on specific menu items may apply.

Military discounts vary a lot by store, since Papa John's doesn't run one official nationwide policy — see our military discount guide for what's actually confirmed. Students get a stronger, genuinely active deal in the UK, but nothing nationwide in the US — full details in our student discount guide.

How to Use a Papa John's Coupon Code

  1. Build your order on papajohns.com or in the Papa John's app.
  2. Go to checkout and review your cart.
  3. Find the promo code field near the order summary.
  4. Enter the code exactly as shown, with no extra spaces, and apply it.
  5. Confirm the discount appears in your total before paying.
  6. Complete checkout and keep your confirmation.

Website vs. app: the Papa John's app sometimes surfaces store-specific deals that don't appear on the website, so it's worth checking both before you order.

Before You Enter a Code

  • Check your cart meets any minimum order requirement
  • Confirm your local store participates in the offer
  • Check specialty or already-discounted items aren't excluded
  • Clear any conflicting reward or credit already on the order
  • Enter the code exactly as shown, with no extra spaces

If Your Papa John's Promo Code Won't Apply

  • It may have expired or was tied to a limited window
  • Your cart may not meet that code's order minimum
  • Your items may be excluded (specialty or discounted items often are)
  • The code may be store-specific and your location isn't participating
  • A different code or reward may already be applied to the order

Papa John's One Code Per Order Rule

Only one Papa John's coupon code applies per order. You can't combine SM25 with a teacher discount or a rewards offer. If more than one fits your situation, compare the actual dollar savings on your cart rather than picking by percentage alone.

  • Two percentage-off codes can't be stacked on the same order
  • A public code and an ID-based discount (teacher, military, student) don't combine
  • A promo code and a Papa Rewards account-only offer don't combine, though you still earn points
  • BOGO promotions and percentage-off codes are mutually exclusive — it's one or the other
  • Papa Dough credit is the exception: it typically applies on top of a code since it functions as account balance, not a second code

Papa John's Promo Code FAQ

Is there a Papa John's code for 30% off?

Yes, but it's tied to email/text signup for new customers. Public codes for existing customers currently top out around 25% off.

Is the Papa John's $9.99 pizza a promo code?

No. It's a Papa Rewards account-only offer, not a public promo code, so you need to be signed into a Rewards account to see it.

Is there a set BOGO day at Papa John's?

There's no fixed nationwide BOGO day. BOGO-style savings show up occasionally through Papa Pairings bundles or seasonal promotions instead — see our BOGO deal guide for how it works.

Does a promo code cover the delivery fee?

No. Delivery fees are billed separately and aren't reduced by percentage-off codes like SM25 or TAKE25DEAL.

Is there an offer for first-time orders?

Yes. Email/text signup gives new customers up to 30% off, which is typically the largest single-use discount available.

Can I combine more than one promo code?

No. Only one code applies per order, so compare the dollar value of each offer against your cart before choosing one.

Do these codes work on carryout orders?

Yes. SM25 and TAKE25DEAL both apply to carryout, and carryout often nets slightly more savings since there's no delivery fee involved.

Can I use a promo code alongside Papa Rewards?

You'll still earn Rewards points on the order, but you can't stack a separate rewards-only discount with a public code on the same order.

Do these Papa John's pizza coupons apply to specialty pizzas?

Sometimes they're excluded or partially excluded. Specialty and already-discounted items are the most common exclusions, so check your cart total before paying.

How often does Papa John's release new codes?

It varies, but new codes tend to appear a few times a month, with extra activity around holidays and seasonal menu launches.

Where can I find offers straight from Papa John's?

The Papa John's app and website Offers/Deals page, plus any email or text offers after signing up for alerts.
