How Papa Rewards points & Papa Dough work

Papa Rewards is Papa John's free loyalty program: you earn 1 point for every $1 spent, and every 10 points automatically converts into $1 of Papa Dough that you can apply toward almost any order. This page breaks down exactly how points are earned, how Papa Dough gets redeemed, and the expiration rules that trip people up most.

Everything here reflects Papa John's own Papa Rewards terms as published on their site. Program details can be updated by Papa John's at any time, so if something here doesn't match what you see in your account, your account is the accurate source.

How to join

Create an account at papajohns.com/rewards and accept the program terms. There's no fee and no purchase requirement to sign up, you just need an account to start earning on your next order.

How you actually earn points

The earning mechanics differ slightly depending on how you order, though the rate is the same either way.

Ordering online or through the app

Log into your account before or during checkout, and you'll automatically earn 1 point for every whole dollar spent, not counting tax, tip, or delivery fees. Amounts over a whole dollar round down, so a $19.60 order earns 19 points, not 20. Points show up in your account by the next business day.

Ordering in-store or by phone

At participating locations, give the phone number linked to your Papa Rewards account when you order. If the store can't find your number, log into your account and confirm your phone number is correct and current. The earn rate and rounding rules are identical to online orders.

The 12-digit code on your receipt

If you weren't logged into your account, or weren't yet a member, when you placed an order, your receipt may show a 12-digit code at the bottom. This lets you claim the points from that specific order after the fact.

  • Sign up for or log into your account, then click "Claim Points" on the website or "Request Points" in the app.
  • Enter the 12-digit code exactly as printed.
  • The code is single-use and can only be claimed by the person who actually placed the order.
  • It expires three days after you receive it, so this isn't something to save for later.
  • If you're already a member and were logged in when you ordered, you won't see this code, since your points were credited automatically.

How Papa Dough redemption works

  1. Papa Dough builds up automatically as your points cross each 10-point threshold, you don't need to manually convert anything.
  2. Log into your account to see your current Papa Dough balance under the My Papa Rewards tab.
  3. Start a new order and click through to checkout, where you can apply available Papa Dough to reduce what you owe.
  4. The amount you can apply depends on your order total and your balance, up to a maximum of $100 per order.

Papa Dough reduces your bill, it isn't a gift card and can't be withdrawn as cash or applied as a flat partial payment beyond what your order and balance allow.

Expiration rules, and why points and Papa Dough are different

This is the part that catches most people off guard, since points and Papa Dough run on separate clocks.

Expires when
Papa Rewards points12 months of no earning or redeeming activity on the account
Papa Dough (from points)60 days after you receive it, active account or not
Papa Dough (gifted or from a promotion)May expire in fewer than 60 days; the exact date is shown when it's issued

In practice, this means your points balance can sit safely for up to a year as long as you keep using the account, but once points convert into Papa Dough, that Papa Dough has a hard 60-day clock regardless of how active you are. Letting Papa Dough sit unused past 60 days forfeits it.

What counts toward points and what doesn't

  • Counts: the pre-tax, pre-tip, pre-delivery-fee cost of your order, including orders paid for with a gift card.
  • Doesn't count: tax, tip, delivery fees, or Papa Priority charges.
  • Doesn't count: buying a gift card itself online. You earn points when you spend on food, not when you purchase the gift card.
  • Does count: tax-exempt orders, but only when placed in-store rather than online.

Papa Dough and reward redemptions can generally be combined with other promotional offers, though individual promotions may carry their own restrictions that override this. Papa Rewards FAQ

How many points do I need for $1 in Papa Dough?

10 points converts automatically into $1 of Papa Dough. You don't need to redeem points manually, the conversion happens on its own.

Do Papa Rewards points expire?

Yes, but only after 12 months with no earning or redemption activity on your account. Staying active, even with small orders, keeps your points balance alive.

Does Papa Dough expire faster than points?

Yes. Papa Dough expires 60 days after you receive it regardless of account activity, which is a much shorter window than the 12-month rule for unconverted points.

Is there a limit on how much Papa Dough I can use per order?

Yes, up to $100 in Papa Dough per order. Anything above that is paid by another method.

Can I combine Papa Dough with a promo code?

In most cases yes, though some specific promotions may restrict combining offers. Check the terms of the specific deal if you're unsure.

What happens to my Papa Dough if I cancel an order?

If your order is cancelled at the store level, any Papa Dough applied to it is returned to your account immediately.

Do I earn points on delivery fees or tips?

No. Points are earned only on the food and item total, not tax, tip, or delivery fees.

Can I merge two Papa Rewards accounts?

No. Accounts can't be combined, so you'll need to pick one account to keep earning on going forward.

What's the 12-digit code on my receipt for?

It lets you claim points for an order you placed without being logged in or before joining. It's single-use and expires three days after you get the receipt.

Do I earn points if I pay with a gift card?

Yes, orders paid for with a gift card earn points normally. What doesn't earn points is purchasing the gift card itself online.

Papa Rewards: Bottom Line

Papa Rewards is straightforward once the two clocks are clear: points can wait up to 12 months as long as your account stays active, but the moment points convert into Papa Dough, that Papa Dough needs to be used within 60 days or it's gone. Keep that distinction in mind and the program mostly runs itself.
